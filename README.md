# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project explores retail sales data using Python to identify sales trends, customer demographics, product performance, and revenue patterns.

The analysis focuses on turning raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making.

## Project Objective
To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales transactions and uncover patterns in customer behaviour, product performance, and sales revenue.

## Dataset Overview
- **Total Records:** 5,000 transactions
- **Total Columns:** 14
- **Period Covered:** January 2023 – December 2024
- **Data Format:** CSV

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

1. **Data Inspection:** Examined dataset structure, data types, missing values, and duplicate records.
2. **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculated mean, median, mode, and standard deviation for numerical variables.
3. **Time Series Analysis:** Examined monthly and quarterly sales revenue trends.
4. **Customer Demographics:** Analysed customer age groups and gender distribution.
5. **Product Analysis:** Identified the top 10 best-selling products and compared revenue across product categories.
6. **Correlation Analysis:** Used a correlation heatmap to examine relationships between numerical variables.
7. **Sales Channel Analysis:** Compared revenue generated through Online, In-Store, and Mobile App channels.

## Key Findings

- Online sales generated the highest revenue among the three sales channels.
- Home was the highest-revenue product category.
- Tennis Racket recorded the highest total units sold.
- The third quarter of 2023 recorded the highest quarterly revenue.
- Unit price and quantity showed positive relationships with total revenue.
- Sales revenue fluctuated throughout the period rather than showing consistent growth.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the factors driving online sales and apply successful strategies to other channels.
2. Improve inventory planning for high-demand products to reduce the risk of stock shortages.
3. Review monthly and quarterly sales patterns to support better promotional and inventory planning.
4. Investigate the performance of lower-revenue product categories to identify improvement opportunities.
5. Monitor pricing and sales volume to better understand their relationship with revenue.

## Project Files

- `Retail_Sales_EDA.ipynb` – Jupyter Notebook containing the analysis, visualisations, and findings.
- `sales_data.csv` – Dataset used for the analysis.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Python for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualisation, and business insight generation. It highlights how data analysis can help businesses understand sales performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
